Lunch Menu

An organic, freshly prepared lunch every day — with a vegetarian option, an allergy-friendly station, and healthy snacks throughout the day.

Every day we serve two mains (one with a vegetarian or vegan option), a rotating set of sides, and fresh fruit. A Daily Alternative Station is always available for allergies, dietary needs, or selective eaters, and every Friday there's a fresh fruit & dip station.

Prefer to pack? Your child is welcome to bring a labeled lunch — we keep it refrigerated. Please make sure any allergies or dietary needs are noted in your enrollment forms.
