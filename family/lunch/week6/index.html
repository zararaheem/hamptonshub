Week 6 Lunch Menu

Week of Aug 3

We’ve partnered with Insatiable Eats as our lunch service provider. Each day brings two mains — one with a vegetarian or vegan option — plus a rotating set of sides and fresh fruit. A Daily Alternative Station is always available for allergies, dietary needs, or selective eaters, and every Friday there’s a fresh fruit & dip station.

Prefer to pack? Your child is welcome to bring a labeled lunch — we keep it refrigerated. Please make sure any allergies or dietary needs are noted in your enrollment forms.
